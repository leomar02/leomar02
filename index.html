Dress code

Black tie

  • TuxedoBlack or midnight blue
  • Cocktail dressSleek, in a formal fabric
  • Floor-length gownSilk, velvet, chiffon or satin
  • Evening jumpsuitOr a dinner suit

For men

Jacket
A black or midnight blue dinner jacket (tuxedo) with satin or grosgrain lapels, shawl or peak.
Trousers
Matching black or midnight blue trousers with a satin or grosgrain stripe down the outer leg seam, no cuffs.
Shirt
A crisp white formal dress shirt with French cuffs and a bib, Marcella or hidden-placket front, worn with cufflinks and black studs.
Neckwear
A black silk or satin bow tie, preferably self-tied.
Waist
A black satin cummerbund (pleats facing up) or a low-cut evening waistcoat.
Footwear
Polished black patent leather Oxfords or court pumps, with over-the-calf black silk socks.
Please avoid
Business suits, regular neckties, belts, brown shoes, and coloured or patterned shirts.

For women

Attire
A floor-length evening gown or a sleek, luxe cocktail dress in a formal fabric such as silk, velvet, chiffon or satin. Tailored evening jumpsuits and dinner suits are also welcome.
Accessories
Refined jewelry and a chic clutch or small evening bag.
Footwear
Dressy heels or polished formal flats suited to the venue terrain.
Please avoid
Casual sundresses, oversized daytime bags and casual footwear.
